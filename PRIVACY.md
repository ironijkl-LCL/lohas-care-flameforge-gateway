# Privacy Policy for FlameForge Pro

Last updated: September 8, 2026

FlameForge Pro ("the App") is developed as an independent thought-refining and structured writing tool. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
FlameForge Pro does not collect, harvest, track, or share any personal identity information. All generated text, prompt setups, thought frameworks, and user inputs are strictly processed locally or via user-authorized direct API connections. 

### 2. Local Storage and Sync
All draft records, historical data, and application settings reside exclusively on your local device. If enabled, synchronization utilizes your private Apple iCloud container, and no external private servers have access to your content.

### 3. Network and Third-Party Services
The App relies strictly on standard secure HTTPS protocols for network communications. We do not integrate third-party ad networks, commercial trackers, or behavioral analytics SDKs that profile users.

### 4. Children's Privacy
FlameForge Pro does not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13.

### 5. Policy Updates
Any future modifications to this policy will be reflected directly on this page alongside version updates.
